# CONTEXT — equipment-rental vocabulary

<!-- graffino-agentic:begin v1 -->
> The approved terms for this project. Use each term exactly as defined, in the part
> of speech given. Never swap in a synonym; never use a word on an _Avoid_ line.
<!-- graffino-agentic:end -->

**Equipment Model** (noun) — An abstract catalog definition of a product with specifications, tiered pricing, and photos.
_Avoid_: item type, product template

**Physical Unit** (noun) — A discrete physical hardware asset identified by a unique serial number and inventory barcode.
_Avoid_: physical piece, device, copy

**Rental** (noun) — A transaction and active agreement where a customer rents equipment for a defined period.
_Avoid_: lease, booking, borrow

**Customer** (noun) — An individual or business entity who rents equipment.
_Avoid_: client, buyer, user

**Kit Checklist** (noun) — The mandatory inventory list of bundled accessories (batteries, cables, charger, bag) verified at handoff and return.
_Avoid_: parts list, packing list

**Maintenance Window** (noun) — A mandatory turnover buffer period scheduled between rentals for unit inspection, sensor cleaning, and charging.
_Avoid_: cleaning time, cooldown

**Damage Report** (noun) — An incident record created during check-in documenting physical/functional defects with attached photographs.
_Avoid_: broken report, penalty sheet

**Deposit** (noun) — A monetary guarantee held during the rental period to cover potential damages, accessory losses, or late returns.
_Avoid_: bond, bail, pledge
